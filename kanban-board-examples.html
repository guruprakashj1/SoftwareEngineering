Implementation Tips

  • Start simple and evolve the board based on team needs
  • Keep WIP limits visible and enforced
  • Use colors consistently for priority levels
  • Include clear policies for moving cards
  • Make blocked items highly visible
  • Review and adjust swimlanes as needed
  • Consider adding cycle time indicators
